Questions

Frequently asked.

What is Cosmicore?
Cosmicore is a space-themed productivity app where every focus session you complete forges a unique celestial object into your personal galaxy. It combines a focus timer, task manager, habit tracker, calendar, and notes — all in one beautiful cosmic universe.
Is it really free?
Yes — the Explorer tier is completely free, forever. You get unlimited focus sessions (up to 25 min), task management, calendar, habits, and the first 20 celestial objects. No credit card required to start.
What does Cosmic Pro add?
Pro unlocks the full galaxy of 96 celestial objects, custom focus durations (any length), levels 6–10, unlimited habits and notes, body doubling (see who's forging live), advanced analytics, and collaboration on tasks and notes. At £4.99/month or £24.99/year.
Do I need to install an app?
No — Cosmicore runs entirely in your browser at app.cosmicore.app. You can also install it as a PWA on any device for a native app experience. Cosmicore is available on Google Play for Android. iOS App Store version is coming soon.
Does it work offline?
Partially. The app loads offline after your first visit thanks to the service worker. However, focus sessions, task saves, and galaxy updates require a connection to sync to your account.
How do push notifications work?
Cosmicore sends push notifications for event reminders, task deadlines, streak alerts, habit nudges, and achievement unlocks — even when the app is closed. On iOS, you need to add Cosmicore to your Home Screen first to receive push notifications.
Can I cancel my Pro subscription?
Yes, anytime. Go to Settings → Subscription in the app and cancel with one tap. You keep Pro access until the end of your billing period. No questions asked.
I have a question or need support.
Email us at hellocosmicore@gmail.com — we respond within 24 hours.
